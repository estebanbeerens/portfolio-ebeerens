Privacy notice

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This is my personal portfolio. I use submitted details to reply to messages and, if you choose, to know who is interested in my CV. I do not sell these details or use them for marketing or unrelated commercial purposes.

Who is responsible?

Esteban Beerens is the data controller. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@ebeerens.com.

Contact messages

The contact form collects your name, email, optional organization, subject and message. I use these to read and respond to your inquiry. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in handling correspondence initiated by you. Required fields are needed to send a message through this form. Messages are automatically deleted from the live database after twelve calendar months, or sooner following an applicable deletion request.

CV downloads

You can download my CV without giving your name or email. If you choose to share your name, email or reason, I store those optional details only with your consent, together with the submission time and a record of that consent. The purpose is to know who is interested in my CV, not to send marketing. These details are automatically deleted from the live database after six calendar months. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing me, without affecting your ability to download the CV or the lawfulness of earlier processing.

I also count issued download links using timestamps, without linking those counts to your submitted details, IP address or browser. These events are removed after twelve calendar months. Your details are not included in the PDF, and the CV is downloaded directly rather than emailed.

Security and service providers

The site uses Oracle Cloud hosting and Cloudflare for network security, bot verification and storage. These providers can process technical information such as your IP address and request metadata. Cloudflare Turnstile checks form submissions for abuse; verification tokens are not saved with your message or CV request. My legal basis for necessary security processing is the legitimate interest in keeping the site safe and available.

Provider processing can involve locations outside the European Economic Area. Contact me for details of the applicable locations and transfer safeguards. Further information is available in the Cloudflare privacy policy and Oracle privacy policy.

Storage and backups

Submissions are stored in a restricted database accessible to me and the infrastructure providers needed to operate the site. Deleted data may remain in restricted disaster-recovery backups for up to thirty additional days. It is not used for other purposes, and expiry and earlier deletions must be reapplied before a restored database is brought online. Minimal record identifiers used to reconcile deletions are kept separately for up to thirty-one days. Site-controlled security logs are retained for up to thirty days; infrastructure providers apply their own security-log policies.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and request portability for data processed on consent where applicable. You can also withdraw CV logging consent. Email privacy@ebeerens.com; I may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity. I normally respond within one month, subject to legally permitted extensions.

You have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the country where you live or work, or where you believe an infringement occurred. The European Data Protection Board directory lists the authorities.
