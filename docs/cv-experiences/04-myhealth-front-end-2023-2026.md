## Full description (EN)

MyHealth is the digital health portal of the Federal Public Service for Health. I worked on the same project from November 2023 to June 2024 and returned in January 2025; the June–December 2024 pause was caused by budget constraints. When I returned, I took on a lead front-end role.

With my team, I built the complete MyHealth web portal from scratch. I translated designs into Angular, developed the front-end architecture, features and reusable web components, and coordinated a MyHealth team of three to four developers. We also worked with other development teams of similar size. As part of the portal, we developed and maintained eight web-component libraries for use in other applications.

My Health Design Kit was the portal’s design system, based on Angular Material with custom overrides for most components. It went through several iterations and was at version 5.x when the project ended in August 2026. Accessibility improvements were measured through WCAG 2.2 AA audits.

As a project team, we also delivered technical and data integrations with health services and partner applications, including:

- **eHealth platform:** I.AM authentication, Consent, Therapeutic Relations, Exclusions, Attribute Authority, MetaHub and other foundational services.
- **Vitalink and Departement Zorg:** KMEHR and FHIR exchange for Sumehr, vaccinations and population screening.
- **Hubs and health vaults:** CoZo, VZN KU Leuven, RSW and RSB/Abrumet.
- **RIZIV/VIDIS:** web components for medication, prescriptions, dispensed medicines and medication schedules.
- **UHMEP/eReferral** for digital referral prescriptions; **MAGS**, the MijnGezondheid mobile app and consumer of MyHealth web components.
- **HealthPro** for searching and selecting healthcare providers; **National Register/RRNConsult**.
- **Federal Public Service for Health** for CMS, content, translations and portal management; **Smals** for components, integrations and the design system.
- **Health insurance funds, MyHealthViewer and Intermut** for data integration and access.

The portal and these integrations were team deliveries. My individual contribution focused primarily on front-end development, technical coordination and collaboration with the development teams involved.
